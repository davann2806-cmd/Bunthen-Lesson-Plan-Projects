# Claude Slash Commands Reference

Open terminal in this folder and run `claude`, then you can invoke any of the following slash commands:

1. `/slide1`
   Title slide: Teacher name, lesson title, Preah Sisowath High School.

2. `/slide2`
   Homework correction (review of old lesson: triangle congruence SAS counterexample).

3. `/slide3`
   Angle construction using a protractor (5 steps with ruler & pencil animation).

4. `/slide4`
   Positions of alternate interior angles (parallel lines, transversal, callouts).

5. `/slide5`
   Properties of alternate interior angles (angles are equal: 60° and 120°).

6. `/slide6`
   Angles adjacent to a side in a triangle (side AB, adjacent angles Â & B̂, opposite angle Ĉ).

7. `/slide7`
   New lesson: ASA congruence condition (question, two triangles, superposition animation, theorem).

8. `/slide8`
   Consequences of ASA (structured table: hypothesis, conclusion, consequences).

9. `/slide9`
   Model problem (comparing ΔAED and ΔBEC, deducing ED = EC).

10. `/slide10`
   Practice exercise (proving ΔABC ≅ ΔAED and AD = AC).

11. `/slide11`
   Homework assignment & teacher closing note (triangle with angle bisector, proving congruences).

*Claude autonomously decides the optimal slide count and animation frames for maximum clarity and engagement.*
