## 2. Peer-Reviewed Pedagogical Evidence (No-LCD, Worksheet-Driven)
1. **Zazkis, R., & Kontorovich, I. (2016)**. *A curious case of superscript $(-1)$: ProspectiveSecondary teachers' explanations of $a^{-1}$ and scientific notation*. Educational Studies in Mathematics / related multiplicative structure research.
   - **Finding**: Learners frequently confuse non-canonical forms ($49 \times 10^7$ or $0.732 \times 10^{-6}$) with canonical $A \times 10^n$ ($1 \le A < 10$) and reverse the sign of $n$ when relying on rote "left/right" mnemonics.
2. **Magnitude-Anchor Strategy (NCTM / Steinle & Stacey, 2004)**:
   - **Classroom Translation**: Replacing fragile "left = $+$, right = $-$" mnemonics with a **2-Check Worksheet Protocol**: (1) **Coefficient Gate** ($1 \le A < 10$: exactly 1 non-zero digit before the decimal point), and (2) **Magnitude Anchor** (Original number $\ge 10 \implies n > 0$; Original number between $0$ and $1 \implies n < 0$).
3. **Split-Factor Grouping for Operations**: Grouping $\left(\frac{A_1}{A_2}\right) \times 10^{n_1 - n_2}$ and showing explicit manual long division (`143.8 ÷ 127 = 1.13...`) enables rural classrooms without calculators or LCDs to master planetary comparison problems accurately.
