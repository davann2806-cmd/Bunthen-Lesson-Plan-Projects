# Research Brief — `២.៤.៤ ស្វ័យគុណនៃ 10` (`POW10`, Grade 8 Lesson 2)

## 1. Textbook Scope & MoEYS Alignment
- **Textbook Pages**: Grade 8 Mathematics (`មេរៀនទី២៖ ស្វ័យគុណ`), Section `4.4. ស្វ័យគុណនៃ 10` (pp. 19–21 top) & Exercise `#10` (p. 26).
- **Core Concepts**:
  - Positive powers $10^n = 1\underbrace{00\dots 0}_{n\text{ zeros}}$ ($n\in\mathbb{Z}^+$), $10^0 = 1$, and negative powers $10^{-n} = \frac{1}{10^n} = 0.\underbrace{00\dots 01}_{n\text{ decimal places}}$.
  - Multiplying and dividing decimals by $10^n$ (`3.75 × 10^4`, `128.5 ÷ 10^2`, `62.013 × 10^5`, `0.57 ÷ 10^5`, `762.0213 × 10^5`, `34.57 ÷ 10^5`).
  - Metric unit conversions (`cm → m`, `g → kg`, `ml → l`) expressed as simplified fractions and decimals.

## 2. Peer-Reviewed Pedagogical Evidence (No-LCD, Worksheet-Driven)
1. **Resnick, L. B., Nesher, P., Leonard, F., Magone, M., Omanson, S., & Peled, I. (1989)**. *Conceptual bases of arithmetic errors: The case of decimal fractions*. Journal for Research in Mathematics Education, 20(1), 8–27. https://doi.org/10.2307/749095
   - **Finding**: Students overgeneralize the whole-number "append $n$ zeros" rule to decimals (e.g., $3.75 \times 10^4 \to 3.750000$). Explicit place-value shifting arches prevent whole-number rule interference.
2. **Steinle, V., & Stacey, K. (2004)**. *A longitudinal study of students' understanding of decimal notation: An overview and refined results*. Mathematics Education Research Journal, 16(1), 3–20.
   - **Finding**: Students confuse negative exponents of $10$ ($10^{-3} = 0.001$) with negative numbers ($-1000$ or $-30$). A symmetric $\div 10$ place-value ladder across $10^0 = 1$ proves $10^{-n} > 0$.
3. **Hiebert, J., & Wearne, D. (1986)**. *Procedures over concepts: The acquisition of decimal number knowledge*. In Conceptual and Procedural Knowledge: The Case of Mathematics (pp. 199–223).
   - **Classroom Translation**: Pairing concrete metric benchmarks (`1 cm = 1/100 m = 0.01 m`, `1 g = 1/1000 kg = 0.001 kg`) with curved decimal-hop tracks on a fill-in worksheet builds conceptual-procedural fluency without calculators or slides.
