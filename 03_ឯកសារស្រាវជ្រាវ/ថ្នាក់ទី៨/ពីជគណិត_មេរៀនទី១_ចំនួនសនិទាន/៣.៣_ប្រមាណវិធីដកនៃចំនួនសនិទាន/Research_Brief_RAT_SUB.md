# Research Brief: Grade 8 Lesson 1 — 3.3 Subtraction of Rational Numbers (`RAT_SUB`)

## 1. Textbook Scope (MoEYS Grade 8 Mathematics, pp. 7–8 & p. 12)
- **3.3 Subtraction of Rational Numbers (`ប្រមាណវិធីដកនៃចំនួនសនិទាន`, pp. 7–8)**:
  - Introductory examples (p. 7): ក) $\frac{9}{10}-\frac{7}{10}=\frac{2}{10}=\frac{1}{5}$; ខ) $\frac{5}{4}-\frac{7}{8}=\frac{10-7}{8}=\frac{3}{8}$; គ) $-\frac{5}{6}-\left(-\frac{7}{3}\right)=\frac{-5}{6}+\frac{7}{3}=\frac{-5+14}{6}=\frac{9}{6}=\frac{3}{2}$.
  - General rule box (`ជាទូទៅ`, p. 7): $\frac{a}{b}-\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad-bc}{bd}$; Remark (`សម្គាល់`): $\frac{a}{b}-\frac{c}{b}=\frac{a-c}{b}$.
  - Worked Example 1 (`លំហាត់គំរូទី 1`, p. 7): ក) $\frac{3}{5}-\frac{7}{10}=\frac{6-7}{10}=\frac{-1}{10}$; ខ) $\frac{9}{12}-\frac{2}{3}=\frac{9-8}{12}=\frac{1}{12}$.
  - Worked Example 2 (`លំហាត់គំរូទី 2`, p. 8): Seminar participants — musicians $\frac{1}{4}$, artists $\frac{2}{5}$, dancers $\frac{1}{10}$, remainder writers: $1 - \left(\frac{1}{4}+\frac{2}{5}+\frac{1}{10}\right) = 1 - \frac{15}{20} = 1 - \frac{3}{4} = \frac{1}{4}$.
  - Practice (`ប្រតិបត្តិ`, p. 8): ក) $-\frac{5}{24}-\frac{7}{30}=\frac{-25-28}{120}=\frac{-53}{120}$; ខ) $\frac{1}{15}-\frac{27}{50}=\frac{10-81}{150}=\frac{-71}{150}$.
  - End-of-lesson exercises (p. 12): `#9ខ` $\left(-3\frac{1}{8}\right)-\left(-2\frac{5}{9}\right)=\frac{-41}{72}$, `#10ង` $-\frac{11}{8}+\frac{21}{4}-\frac{17}{16}=\frac{45}{16}$.

## 2. Empirical Research Foundations (Verified Open-Access Literature in `papers/`)
1. **Jarrah, A. M., Wardat, Y., & Gningue, S. (2022).** *Misconception on addition and subtraction of fractions in seventh-grade middle school students.* EURASIA Journal of Mathematics, Science and Technology Education, 18(6), em2115. (`01_Jarrah2022_SeventhGradeFractionMisconceptions.pdf`)
   - **Finding**: Students frequently subtract smaller numerators from larger ones regardless of order ($\frac{3}{5}-\frac{7}{10}\to\frac{7-6}{10}=\frac{1}{10}$) or subtract denominators directly ($\frac{a-c}{b-d}$).
2. **Retnowati, S., Amin, S. M., & Imah, E. M. (2018).** *The role of refutational text as a conceptual change effort to fix the misconception on addition and subtraction of integers.* Journal of Physics: Conference Series, 1108, 012117. (`02_Retnowati2018_RefutationalAdditionSubtraction.pdf`)
   - **Finding**: Refutational comparison boxes that explicitly contrast $-a - b = -(a+b)$ against $-a - (-b) = -a + b$ prevent students from misapplying "two negatives make a positive" to expressions like $-\frac{5}{24}-\frac{7}{30}$.
3. **hakim / TEM Journal (2021).** *Students' misconceptions on negative fractions and signed rational operations.* (`03_misconceptions_2021_tem_negative_frac.pdf`)
   - **Finding**: Rewriting rational subtraction as "adding the additive inverse" ($\frac{a}{b}-\frac{c}{d}=\frac{a}{b}+\left(-\frac{c}{d}\right)$) unifies sign management across negative fractions and mixed numbers.
4. **Wesenberg, L., et al. (2022).** *Correct and erroneous worked examples in rational number learning.* (`04_Wesenberg2022_CorrectErroneousWorkedExamples.pdf`)
   - **Finding**: Side-by-side correct vs. erroneous worked examples significantly reduce sign-drop errors in multi-step fraction subtraction.
5. **Yeo, D. J., et al. (2019).** *Finger tracing over worked examples enhances learning of mathematical procedures.* (`05_Yeo2019_FingerTracingWorkedExamples.pdf`)
   - **Finding**: Monochrome bracket/circle cues (`[គូសរង្វង់សោសញ្ញា -(- ) -> +]`) lower working-memory load on B&W paper worksheets without needing color markers or slides.

## 3. Zero-LCD 5Es Pedagogical Translation (50 Minutes)
- **E1 (3 min)**: Directed Elevation/Sign Paradox on $-\frac{5}{6}-\left(-\frac{7}{3}\right)$ — does removing a negative debt/depth make the result smaller ($-\frac{19}{6}$) or larger ($+\frac{3}{2}$)?
- **E2 (11 min)**: `POE 1` Directed Number-Line & Double-Sign Lock `[-(- ) -> +]` (`ឧទាហរណ៍ ក–គ` & `គំរូទី១ ក`, p. 7); `POE 2` 20-Cell Whole-Unit Complement Bar (`1 - sum`, `គំរូទី២` Seminar Problem, p. 8).
- **E3 (6 min)**: Formal rule $\frac{a}{b}-\frac{c}{d}=\frac{ad-bc}{bd}$, non-commutativity of subtraction, and B&W Warrant Bank `①–⑤`.
- **E4 (10 min)**: Worked-example investigation of `#9ខ` $\left(-3\frac{1}{8}\right)-\left(-2\frac{5}{9}\right)$ and `គំរូទី២` complement problem + `EIA Looking-Back` contrasting $-a-(-b)$ vs. $-a-b$.
- **E5 (10 min)**: Partner `Pause-Review` on `ប្រតិបត្តិ` p. 8 ($-\frac{5}{24}-\frac{7}{30}$ and $\frac{1}{15}-\frac{27}{50}$), 4-foil Diagnostic Check (`A–D`), and 60-second Exit Ticket.
