# Research Brief: Grade 8 Lesson 1 — 3.1 & 3.2 Addition of Rational Numbers & Properties (`RAT_ADD`)

## 1. Textbook Scope (MoEYS Grade 8 Mathematics, pp. 5–7)
- **3.1 Addition of Rational Numbers (`ប្រមាណវិធីបូក`, pp. 5–6)**:
  - General rule: $\frac{a}{b} + \frac{c}{d} = \frac{ad + bc}{bd}$ (and LCD/PPCM equivalence); remark $\frac{a}{b} + \frac{c}{b} = \frac{a+c}{b}$.
  - Worked examples: $\frac{9}{5}+\frac{7}{5}=\frac{16}{5}$, $3.5+\frac{79}{10}=\frac{57}{5}$; signed denominators $\frac{-3}{8}+\frac{5}{-8}=-1$, $\frac{-3}{5}+\frac{4}{-7}=\frac{-41}{35}$; mixed numbers $(-3\frac{3}{5})+(3\frac{7}{4})=\frac{23}{20}$, three-term sum $(\frac{-1}{4})+\frac{5}{6}+\frac{7}{3}=\frac{35}{12}$, decimals $437.56+127.95=565.51$.
- **3.2 Properties of Addition (`លក្ខណៈ`, pp. 6–7)**:
  - Closure, Commutative (`ត្រឡប់`), Associative (`ផ្គុំ`), Identity $0$, Additive Inverse (`ចំនួនផ្ទុយ` $-\frac{a}{b}$ where $\frac{a}{b}+(-\frac{a}{b})=0$), Cancellation, Double Opposite $-(-\frac{a}{b})=\frac{a}{b}$.
  - Strategic associative grouping: $(\frac{5}{6}+\frac{7}{3})+\frac{5}{3}=\frac{29}{6}$ and $(\frac{5}{7}+\frac{3}{8})+(\frac{17}{8}+\frac{5}{-7})=\frac{5}{2}$.

## 2. Empirical Research Foundations (Verified Open-Access Literature)
1. **Jarrah, A. M., Wardat, Y., & Gningue, S. (2022).** *Misconception on addition and subtraction of fractions in seventh-grade middle school students.* EURASIA Journal of Mathematics, Science and Technology Education, 18(6), em2115. https://doi.org/10.29333/ejmste/12070
   - **Finding**: The dominant persistent error in middle school rational addition is adding numerators and denominators independently ($\frac{a}{b}+\frac{c}{d}=\frac{a+c}{b+d}$) due to treating fractions as pairs of unrelated integers rather than equal-sized unit parts.
2. **Alibali, M. W., & Sidney, P. G. (2015).** *Variability in the natural number bias: Who, when, how, and why.* Learning and Instruction, 37, 56–61.
   - **Finding**: Magnitude estimation prompts ("Can $\frac{1}{2}+\frac{1}{3}$ be $\frac{2}{5} < \frac{1}{2}$?") trigger immediate cognitive conflict against natural-number bias before formal common-denominator rules are stated.
3. **Retnowati, S., Amin, S. M., & Imah, E. M. (2018).** *The role of refutational text as a conceptual change effort to fix the misconception on addition and subtraction of integers.* Journal of Physics: Conference Series, 1108, 012117.
   - **Finding**: Explicit refutational prompts contrasting incorrect sign combinations against directed number-line / zero-pair reasoning significantly outperform rule-only instruction on signed-number addition.
4. **Barbieri, C. A., Booth, J. L., & Chawla, K. (2022).** *Let's be rational: Worked examples supplemented textbooks improve conceptual and fraction knowledge.* Educational Psychology, 43(2–3), 132–152.
   - **Finding**: Pairing printed textbook exercises with structured worked examples and self-explanation prompts significantly raises middle-school students' conceptual and procedural fraction accuracy without requiring digital screens.
5. **Van, L., Mao, S., & Cnudde, V. (2018).** *Improving pedagogical knowledge on rational numbers of Cambodian teachers.* Global Education Review, 5(3), 196–211.
   - **Finding**: In Cambodian lower-secondary classrooms, visual fraction-bar partitioning and benchmark estimation on paper worksheets effectively bridge rote PPCM rules to genuine rational magnitude sense.
